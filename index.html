Yes. Download ELVÉRA and turn on Demo mode to explore every screen with sample data.

That's who we designed it for. Big text, simple words, and family members can see how each other are doing.

Where is my data stored?

Your health data stays on your own phone unless you choose to share it with family. You can turn sharing off at any time.

Is ELVÉRA a medical device?

No. ELVÉRA is for everyday wellness and is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any disease. Always talk with your doctor about your health.
