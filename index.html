Questions

Good to know

Can I try the app before buying?

Yes. Demo mode in the app shows every screen with sample data.

Is it easy for my parents to use?

That's who we designed it for. Big text, simple words, and family members can see how each other are doing.

How do I choose a size?

We suggest your index or middle finger. The right size feels snug without spinning. Measure the inside of a ring that fits you and match its diameter:

SizeInside diameter
What if it doesn't fit, or I change my mind?

Every order comes with 30-day returns. Send the ring back within 30 days of delivery and we'll make it right. Every ring also has a 1-year warranty.

Where is my data stored?

On your own phone. Some data leaves it only if you turn on family sharing, agree to use AI, or press SOS. Your name and age are stored on our server when you join a family group. See our Privacy policy.

Is ELVÉRA a medical device?

No. ELVÉRA is for everyday wellness and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Always talk with your doctor about your health.
