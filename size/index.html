Find your ring size

Print this page, then use either method below. It takes about two minutes.

Before you start: check the print size

Print at 100% ("Actual size", not "Fit to page"). Then lay a credit card on the box. If it matches the box, the sizer is accurate.

Method 1 · You already have a ring that fits (most accurate)

Place the ring on the circles. Your size is the circle that fits just inside the ring, with the line still visible all around.

Method 2 · Paper strip

  1. Cut out the strip along the dashed line.
  2. Wrap it around the finger you will wear the ring on, numbers facing out. Snug, but not tight.
  3. Where the red line meets the strip, read the number. Between two numbers? Choose the larger one.
