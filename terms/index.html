Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. General wellness, not medical care

ELVÉRA's numbers and notes are for general wellness only. ELVÉRA is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease and does not replace a doctor. Please do not use it to decide whether to see a doctor, or to take or stop any medicine. If you feel unwell, see a doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

2. Numbers may be off

Ring numbers are estimates. Fit, temperature, movement and devices can make them inaccurate, late or missing.

3. Notes and family alerts

Notes and family alerts are not alarms. They may be late, may not arrive, or may not be sent. No alert does not mean everything is fine. Please do not rely on ELVÉRA alone for anyone's safety.

4. SOS

SOS only notifies the family members in your family groups. It does not call 911 or any emergency number, does not alert doctors or paramedics, and may be delayed or not delivered. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number directly.

5. AI Coach

AI answers are made by a computer, can be wrong and are not professional advice. Please do not type other people's private information. Using AI is your choice.

6. Exercise

Consider your own condition before exercising. If you have a health condition, ask your doctor first. Stop right away if you feel unwell.

7. Family groups

Only invite people you trust and who agree. Each person decides what to share. Do not use ELVÉRA to follow anyone without their knowledge and consent.

8. Who can use ELVÉRA

You must be at least 13. Minors need a parent's or guardian's permission.

9. Your devices

Los números del anillo son estimaciones. El ajuste, la temperatura, el movimiento y los dispositivos pueden hacer que sean inexactos, lleguen tarde o falten.

Los avisos no son alarmas. Pueden llegar tarde, no llegar o no enviarse. Que no haya aviso no significa que todo esté bien. No dependas solo de ELVÉRA para la seguridad de nadie.
